  • GSTR-1 aur GSTR-3B ki JSON file, GSTR-2B milaan, GSTR-9 summary
  • Stock: kam stock alert, barcode scan aur sticker, batch-expiry, godown, recipe se production
  • Party ka udhaar, WhatsApp payment reminder, part payment, TDS

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kya Bahi bilkul free hai?

Haan. Bill, stock, GST return file, hisaab-kitaab aur PDF – sab muft hai. Koi login ya subscription nahi.

Mera data kahan save hota hai?

Sirf aapke phone ya computer ke browser mein. Kuch bhi online upload nahi hota. Backup file aap khud download karke rakh sakte hain.

Kya isse GST bill aur GSTR-1 file ban sakti hai?

Haan. Pakka (GST tax invoice) aur kachha bill dono bante hain. GSTR-1 aur GSTR-3B ki JSON file aur GSTR-2B milaan bhi hai.

Kya yeh Hindi aur doosri bhashaon mein chalta hai?

Haan – Hindi, Hinglish, English, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Telugu, Odia, Chhattisgarhi aur Assamese.

Kya thermal printer par bill chhap sakta hai?

Haan, 58mm aur 80mm thermal printer aur A4 – teeno. Bill WhatsApp aur email par bhi bhej sakte hain.

Kya stock aur udhaar ka hisaab rakhta hai?

Haan. Bill banate hi stock ghatta hai, kam stock laal ho jata hai, aur party ka udhaar, payment reminder, P&L aur balance sheet apne aap bante hain.
